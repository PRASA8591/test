🔧 Troubleshooting

💾 How do I back up my data?

All data is stored in your browser's IndexedDB. Use browser DevTools → Storage → IndexedDB to view/export data. Future versions will include export features.

🔄 What happens if I clear browser cache?

All POS system data will be lost. It's important to regularly backup your data or export reports before clearing cache.

📱 Does this work on mobile?

Yes! The system is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and desktop browsers. IndexedDB support varies by device/browser.

⚡ Is this really offline?

Yes, 100% offline after loading. Once the page loads, you can disconnect from internet and everything works. All data stays on your device.
